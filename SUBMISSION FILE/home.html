Our Target Audience

Our core audience includes local private vehicle owners, taxi operators, and commuters along Lefiso Main Road seeking reliable automotive care; neighbourhood residents, local workers, and waiting customers looking for fresh street food; and youth, music enthusiasts, and cultural supporters across Lefiso Village and neighbouring Mpumalanga/North-West regions seeking vibrant year-end entertainment and event updates for the Morula Epic 29.

About Our Business

Established in 2021, Morula View Car Wash & Street Food is an established multi-service local hub operating in Lefiso Village (Lefiso Main Road before taxi rank) in the northwestern outskirts of Mpumalanga. The business integrates professional automotive detailing with convenient, high-quality street food catering. Beyond daily services, the venue serves as a prominent community venue for regional culture and entertainment by hosting major annual year-end gatherings, most notably the Morula Epic 29 (Ditrupa Edition). This event showcases traditional Ditrupa cultural performances alongside local musical artists to promote community unity, celebrate local heritage, and inspire through the universal language of music.
